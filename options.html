OpenChamber Go

A peak-hours chip for the DeepSeek off-peak window, plus the Go key switcher and per-subscription quota. It renders inside the OpenChamber page and signs in with that page's own session — no key is stored here.

OpenChamber address

The address you open OpenChamber at, usually your Tailscale one. The chip is injected on that origin only.

Reading a key's quota

OpenChamber reports quota for the active key only. To show each subscription separately, the chip makes each key active for a moment, reads its pool, then puts the previous key back. It does that when you open the chip or press refresh, never on a timer.
